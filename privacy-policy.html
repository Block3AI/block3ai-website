Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

About this policy

Block3AI is a personal developer portfolio showcasing AI, blockchain, Web3, and Web2 coding projects. This policy describes information handled when you browse this website, contact its owner, or use Nova AI. The portfolio owner can be contacted at info@block3ai.com.

Information handled

  • Website visits: the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs to deliver and protect the website.
  • Email: if you contact me, your email address, name if provided, message, and attachments are handled through email services so I can respond and manage the conversation.
  • Nova AI: when you open the chatbot, your browser connects to Botpress. Messages you submit, generated responses, session identifiers, and technical information may be processed and stored by Botpress and the providers used to operate the bot. The bot owner may access conversation records to operate, troubleshoot, and improve the assistant.

Chatbot and third-party services

The embedded Nova AI chatbot loads only after you click its launcher. Opening the separate browser chat takes you to a Botpress-hosted page. Botpress and the bot’s configured AI services process conversation information to provide responses. Read the Botpress Privacy Statement for details about its practices.

Please do not submit passwords, private keys, wallet seed phrases, payment details, sensitive personal information, or confidential material to the chatbot. You can browse the portfolio without using it.

Cookies, browser storage, and analytics

The portfolio’s own code does not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Hosting infrastructure and the chatbot may use cookies or similar technologies, including browser storage, for functionality, session continuity, or security. You can manage cookies and site storage in your browser; clearing or blocking them may affect chat functionality. Minimizing the chat does not delete conversations or disconnect an already loaded chat session.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to deliver and secure the website, respond to inquiries, provide chatbot responses, and diagnose issues. Hosting, email, chatbot, and AI service providers may process information for those purposes. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to address abuse or protect legal rights. This website is not designed to collect information for sale or targeted advertising.

External projects, GitHub repositories, demos, and other linked websites have their own practices and terms. This policy does not cover information you provide directly to those services.

Retention and security

Retention varies by the type of information and service: email records may be kept to handle correspondence, hosting logs follow provider settings, and chatbot records follow the bot configuration and applicable provider retention rules. This website does not promise a fixed deletion period for all services. Contact me if you have questions about a particular record or wish to request deletion.

No website or communication service can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, where privacy rules can differ.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to use the chatbot, limit the information you send, and manage browser storage. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information, or to raise a concern with a privacy regulator. Email info@block3ai.com to make a request. I may need information to verify the request and locate relevant records; some requests may require assistance from a service provider or be subject to legal exceptions.

Children’s privacy

This portfolio and chatbot are not directed to children under 13. Please do not submit personal information about children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me so the matter can be reviewed and appropriate deletion requested.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the website or its services change. The date above identifies the latest revision. For privacy questions, email info@block3ai.com. See also the Terms and Conditions.
